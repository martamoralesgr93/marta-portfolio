02Contexto y problemaContext & problem

El problema no era visualThe problem wasn't visual

La experiencia se había construido con una lógica corporativa e inspiracional, pero la mayor parte del tráfico llegaba desde Paid Media con una intención claramente transaccional: el modelo mental del negocio y el del usuario no coincidían.The experience was built on a corporate, inspirational logic, while most Paid Media traffic arrived with clear transactional intent: a mismatch between the business's mental model and the user's.

La solución tenía queThe solution had to
  • Funcionar en desktop y mobileWork on desktop and mobile

  • Respetar la identidad visualRespect the visual identity
  • Adaptarse a las limitaciones de Adobe Experience ManagerFit Adobe Experience Manager constraints
Mi punto de partidaWhere I started

Here, for the first time, the challenge was to prove with statistics that design moved the business, as the only designer in a backend team.
